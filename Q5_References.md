## Textbook citation correction

The earlier bibliography assigned ER modelling to Chapter 7 of Elmasri and Navathe, *Fundamentals of Database Systems*, seventh edition. The correct chapter is **3: Data Modeling Using the Entity Relationship (ER) Model**; Chapter **9** covers ER/EER-to-relational mapping. The [publisher's contents](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/fundamentals-of-database-systems/P200000003546/9780137502523) support this correction. This is further reading, not a claim that the full textbook was consulted during this session.

## Verification scope

The SQL is targeted at MySQL, not validated as a PostgreSQL script. The regression runner in `tests/validate_mysql.py` creates a disposable local server, loads all tables and sample records, runs the sample queries and checks the corrected integrity rules. These tests cover the identified issues; they do not establish that every possible manufacturing workflow is implemented.
